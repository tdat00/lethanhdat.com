I made three small games for those moments when you want to play and train your powers of observation at the same time. All three can be printed on A4 paper to play together.

- **[Word Maze](/toys/word-search-puzzle.html?lang=en):** Find the hidden words in a letter grid that you create yourself.
- **[Animal Maze](/toys/animal-search-puzzle.html):** Find sequences of animals in a picture grid, which also suits children who cannot read yet.
- **[Number Search](/toys/find-number-puzzle.html):** Test your sharp eyes with a shuffled grid of numbers from a range you choose.

Each game has a language switcher (Vietnamese and English) in its page header.
