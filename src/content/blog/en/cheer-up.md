> He feeds and he curses. It's always the same: as soon as he has fed, he starts cursing. First he curses mid. What's the big deal? Two guys went mid anyway. Then he curses the team. Fine too: the team is everyone, yet nobody. Fed up, he curses this whole SEA server. But everyone on the server thinks, "he must mean everyone except me", and nobody says a word. How infuriating! Yes! This really is infuriating! Infuriating enough to die! So he has to curse whoever isn't feeding together with him. But nobody reacts either. Damn it! Doesn't that waste all his try-harding? Doesn't that make him suffer? Which bastard came up with the low priority queue just to make him suffer like this? Aha. That's right: he keeps cursing, gritting his teeth as he spits out "bobo putang ina mo IceFrog". But who is IceFrog? Only Bruno would know. He doesn't know; not even Steam knows...
